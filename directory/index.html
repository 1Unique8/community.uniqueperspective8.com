What a profile has to hold

A real name, a town, and a concrete craft. A sentence on how the work meets ethical sourcing or stewardship, written from a fact. The Unique Perspective 8 connection only if it happened: a shared table, a material, a date. A link to their own page. If any of those is missing, the profile waits.
