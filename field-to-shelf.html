A stone moves in this order. A skipped step is not a specimen. The reading shelf is here. The shop is the last door, and only if the card still matches.

6. What travels

A piece crosses to the shop only if the number on it matches the card, the face is still readable, and the place named is the public one. A missing card means it does not cross.
